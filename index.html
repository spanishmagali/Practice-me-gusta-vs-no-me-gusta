The Golden Rule: Flip Your Thinking!

Instead of "I like X", Spanish literally says "X pleases me". Use gusta for 1 thing or any action, and gustan for plural things!

💚 Me = To me ❤️ No me = Not to me 🗣️ ¿Te = To you?
